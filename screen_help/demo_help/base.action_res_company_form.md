## Help of an application

Click _Application Help_ at the top of this window: it shows the help of all the screens
of the current application (here _Settings_: the users and the companies), one chapter
per screen, ordered by the lowest sequence of their sections, then by the menu order.
The help of a model is shown once, with its first screen.

### Look of the window

- **Logo**: the logo of the company, at the top of the window
- **Print**: the help, without the buttons and the sidebar
- **Dark mode**: follows the color scheme of the user, when a module provides it
  (`web_dark_mode` of OCA/web, or `web_enterprise`)
