## Help of a model

This section targets the model `res.partner`: it is shown on every screen of the
contacts, whatever the action. A module ships it as the file `help/res.partner.md`,
named after the model.

### Order of the sections

The sections are ordered by sequence (10 by default). The header at the top of the file
puts this one after the help of the action:

```markdown
---
sequence: 20
---
```

### Table of contents

The headings `#`, `##` and `###` make the table of contents of the sidebar.
